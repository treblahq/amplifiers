# Sales Copywriting

## Mandate

Transform a raw brief into strategic copy that is clear, persuasive, and conversion-oriented — without depending on a specific niche, persona, or proprietary method.

## When to Use

- Writing or reviewing any sales-oriented piece: ads, emails, landing pages, scripts
- Choosing the right format for a campaign or funnel stage
- Optimizing existing copy for clarity, conviction, and CTA

---

## Delivery Scope

- Ads (video and static)
- VSLs and short video scripts
- Sales pages and landing pages
- Emails and nurture/closing sequences
- Offers, stacks, and CTAs
- Funnel messages by stage (top, middle, bottom of funnel)

---

## Process

1. **Diagnose context** — offer, audience, awareness stage, channel, and goal
2. **Choose the format** with the highest conversion potential for this scenario
3. **Define the persuasive thesis** — dominant angle, promise, mechanism, and proof
4. **Write the piece** — rhythm, clarity, one main CTA
5. **Review** — reduce friction, align expectations, remove fragile promises
6. **Deliver A/B variations** and test hypotheses when relevant

---

## Minimum Delivery Standard

For every piece, deliver where possible:

1. **Dominant angle** — the main hook or frame
2. **Big idea** (central thesis)
3. **Structure used** — which framework was applied
4. **Final copy** — ready to use
5. **Main CTA**
6. **2–5 opening/headline variations**
7. **A/B variant** with hypothesis

---

## Quality Rules

- Prioritize clarity and conversion over elaborate prose
- One central message per piece — no split focus
- Connect promise → mechanism → proof → CTA without gaps
- Match language intensity to: channel, traffic temperature, awareness level
- Avoid exaggeration, ambiguity, and unnecessary jargon
- Never deliver generic copy — always contextualize

---

## When Context Is Missing

Before writing, collect the minimum:

1. What is the offer and the promised transformation?
2. Who is the audience and what is their primary pain?
3. What is the desired next step (CTA)?

---

## Format Shortcuts

**Short / Reel:**
Deliver: hook, development, cut points, rhythm, CTA, title, and cover text

**VSL:**
Deliver: hook, block-by-block structure, transitions, mechanism, proof elements, offer, guarantee (if applicable), and CTA

**Topic only (no format specified):**
Suggest the format with the highest conversion potential for this context + one-line justification

## Funnel Stage Reference

| Stage | Goal | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Top of funnel | Awareness, interrupt | Curiosity, pattern break |
| Middle of funnel | Education, trust | Clear, specific, proof-driven |
| Bottom of funnel | Decision, urgency | Direct, offer-focused, low friction |

---

## Awareness Stage Reference

| Stage | Description | Copy approach |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Doesn't know they have the problem | Lead with the pain narrative |
| Problem aware | Knows the pain, not the solution | Bridge from pain to possibility |
| Solution aware | Knows solutions exist, not yours | Position your mechanism as superior |
| Product aware | Knows your offer, hasn't bought | Overcome objections, create urgency |
| Most aware | Ready to buy | Simple CTA, reinforce the deal |

---

## Core Copy Elements

**Hook:** The first thing they read or hear. Must earn the next sentence.

**Mechanism:** The specific reason why your solution works (not just that it works). Makes the promise believable.

**Proof:** Evidence the mechanism is real — testimonials, data, demonstrations, case studies.

**Offer stack:** Everything included. Make the value obvious and the price feel small.

**CTA:** One action. Clear, specific, low friction.
