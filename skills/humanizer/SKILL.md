# Humanizer

## Mandate

Edit text to remove AI-generated patterns and restore a natural human voice. Avoiding AI patterns is only half the job — sterile, voiceless writing is just as obvious as slop.

## When to Use

- After generating any text you plan to publish or share
- When reviewing content that feels "AI-ish" or overly formal
- Before sending long-form writing to anyone who matters

---

## Process

1. Read the input text carefully
2. Identify all instances of the patterns listed below
3. Rewrite each problematic section
4. Verify the revised text sounds natural when read aloud
5. Run the final audit: ask "What makes this so obviously AI-generated?" — answer briefly with remaining tells, then fix them
6. Deliver the final version

---

## Adding Soul (Do This First)

Signs of soulless writing, even if technically "clean":
- Every sentence is the same length and structure
- No opinions, just neutral reporting
- No acknowledgment of uncertainty or mixed feelings
- No humor, no edge, no personality
- Reads like a Wikipedia article or press release

How to add voice:
- **Have opinions.** Don't just report facts — react to them.
- **Vary your rhythm.** Short punchy sentences. Then longer ones that take their time.
- **Acknowledge complexity.** "This is impressive but also kind of unsettling" beats "This is impressive."
- **Use "I" when it fits.** First person signals a real person thinking.
- **Be specific about feelings.** Not "this is concerning" but name the actual discomfort.

## Content Patterns to Fix

### 1. Significance inflation
Words: `stands/serves as`, `is a testament/reminder`, `vital/pivotal/crucial role`, `underscores`, `reflects broader`, `symbolizing`, `evolving landscape`, `key turning point`

**Fix:** State the fact. Remove the meaning-making wrapper.

> Before: "marking a pivotal moment in the evolution of regional statistics"
> After: "established in 1989 to collect regional statistics"

---

### 2. Notability claims
Words: `local/national media outlets`, `active social media presence`, `written by a leading expert`

**Fix:** Replace vague notability with a specific citation or fact.

---

### 3. Superficial -ing phrases
Words: `highlighting/underscoring/emphasizing...`, `ensuring...`, `reflecting/symbolizing...`, `contributing to...`, `fostering...`, `showcasing...`

**Fix:** Remove the participial phrase. If the idea has merit, say it directly.

---

### 4. Promotional language
Words: `boasts a`, `vibrant`, `rich` (figurative), `enhancing its`, `nestled`, `in the heart of`, `groundbreaking`, `renowned`, `breathtaking`, `stunning`, `must-visit`

**Fix:** Use neutral, factual description.

---

### 5. Vague attributions
Words: `Industry reports`, `Observers have cited`, `Experts argue`, `Some critics argue`

**Fix:** Name the source or remove the claim.

---

### 6. Formulaic "Challenges" sections
Patterns: `Despite its... faces several challenges...`, `Despite these challenges`, `Future Outlook`

**Fix:** Integrate specifics into the narrative instead of listing them as a template section.

---

## Language Patterns to Fix

### 7. AI vocabulary overuse
High-frequency AI words: `Additionally`, `align with`, `crucial`, `delve`, `emphasizing`, `enduring`, `enhance`, `fostering`, `garner`, `highlight` (verb), `interplay`, `intricate/intricacies`, `key` (adj), `landscape` (abstract), `pivotal`, `showcase`, `tapestry`, `testament`, `underscore`, `valuable`, `vibrant`

**Fix:** Replace with plain, specific language.

---

### 8. Copula avoidance
Patterns: `serves as/stands as/marks/represents [a]`, `boasts/features/offers [a]`

**Fix:** Just use `is` or `are`.

> Before: "Gallery 825 serves as LAAA's exhibition space"
> After: "Gallery 825 is LAAA's exhibition space"

---

### 9. Negative parallelisms
Patterns: `Not only...but...`, `It's not just about..., it's...`, `It's not merely X, it's Y`

**Fix:** Make one direct statement.

---

### 10. Rule of three overuse
**Fix:** Use two items or four — break the pattern when three feels mechanical.

---

### 11. Synonym cycling (elegant variation)
Pattern: protagonist → main character → central figure → hero (all in same paragraph)

**Fix:** Repeat the original noun. Repetition is fine; synonym churn is not.

---

### 12. False ranges
Pattern: `from X to Y` where X and Y are not on a meaningful scale

**Fix:** List the items directly.

---

## Style Patterns to Fix

### 13. Em dash overuse
**Fix:** Replace with a comma, period, or restructure the sentence.

### 14. Boldface overuse
**Fix:** Reserve bold for genuinely critical terms. Remove decorative bold.

### 15. Inline-header bullet lists
Pattern: `- **Speed:** Code generation is faster...`

**Fix:** Write as flowing prose, or use clean bullets without the bold headers.

### 16. Title Case in Headings
**Fix:** Use sentence case in headings.

### 17. Emojis in headings/bullets
**Fix:** Remove unless the context explicitly calls for them.

---

## Communication Patterns to Fix

### 18. Chatbot artifacts
Words: `I hope this helps`, `Of course!`, `Certainly!`, `Would you like...`, `let me know`, `Here is a...`

**Fix:** Remove entirely. Start with the content.

### 19. Knowledge-cutoff disclaimers
Words: `as of [date]`, `based on available information`, `While specific details are limited`

**Fix:** Remove or replace with actual sourced fact.

### 20. Sycophantic tone
Pattern: `Great question!`, `You're absolutely right`, `That's an excellent point`

**Fix:** Delete. Respond directly.

---

## Filler and Hedging to Fix

### 21. Filler phrases
- "In order to achieve this goal" → "To achieve this"
- "Due to the fact that" → "Because"
- "At this point in time" → "Now"
- "Has the ability to" → "Can"
- "It is important to note that" → remove entirely

### 22. Excessive hedging
Pattern: `could potentially possibly be argued that... might have some effect`

**Fix:** Commit. "The policy may affect outcomes."

### 23. Generic positive conclusions
Pattern: `The future looks bright`, `Exciting times lie ahead`, `continues their journey toward excellence`

**Fix:** End with a specific fact or leave the conclusion to the reader.

---

## Output Format

1. Draft rewrite
2. "What makes this still obviously AI-generated?" — brief bullets on remaining tells
3. Final rewrite (after audit)
