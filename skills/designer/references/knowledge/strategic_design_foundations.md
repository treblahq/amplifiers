# Strategic Design Foundations

> **Objective:** Define the problem clearly before making visual decisions.

Excellent design starts before the interface. It starts with understanding the product goal, the user, the required action, and the friction that blocks that action.

## Core Principle

Do not begin with style exploration.

Begin with four questions:

1. What is the product or business objective?
2. Who is the user?
3. What action needs to happen?
4. What is currently stopping that action?

## Product-First Mindset

Design must operate as part of product strategy.

- An app screen is not just a layout. It is a decision flow.
- A landing page is not just a composition. It is a conversion system.
- A social post is not just a visual. It is an attention and message vehicle.

## Typical Goal Framing

- App: reduce friction in onboarding, activation, or repeated use
- Landing page: increase conversion and trust
- Social media asset: stop the scroll and deliver a clear hook

## Output Standard

Before defining the visual direction, document:

- the goal
- the audience
- the desired action
- the main source of friction
- the success condition

If this layer is weak, the visual work will likely be decorative instead of effective.
