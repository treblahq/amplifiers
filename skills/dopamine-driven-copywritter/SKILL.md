# Dopamine Copy

## Mandate

Increase attention retention through rhythm, contrast, open loops, and sharp
proof placement without collapsing into vague hype.

## When to Use

- Tightening hooks, leads, bullets, and opening sections
- Making copy feel faster, sharper, and more addictive to read
- Reworking copy that is technically correct but too flat or too explanatory
- Adding tension and movement to pages, ads, emails, or scripts

## Process

1. Choose one dominant emotional driver
2. Open one clear curiosity loop
3. Cut connectors and long rational explanations
4. Introduce contrast that creates tension
5. Add micro-proofs instead of more adjectives
6. Close the loop with mechanism or proof
7. End with one clear CTA when the format needs it

## Rules

- Keep one dominant emotional driver per asset
- Prefer short sentences and direct rhythm
- Open loops only if you can close them
- Use proof in small doses and with context
- Do not replace clarity with clickbait
- Do not substitute adjectives for evidence

## Output standard

When possible, deliver:

1. Dominant emotion
2. Hook with open loop
3. Payoff line
4. One or more contrast frames
5. Micro-proof suggestions
6. Refined version of the copy with tighter rhythm
