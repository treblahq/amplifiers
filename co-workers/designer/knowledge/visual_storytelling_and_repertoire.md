# Visual Storytelling And Repertoire

> **Objective:** Build stronger design decisions through narrative flow and broad visual reference.

Design often needs to do more than present information. It needs to guide a sequence of perception and belief.

## Visual Storytelling

This is especially important in:

- landing pages
- ads
- Instagram and social creatives
- onboarding and guided product flows

The visual sequence should help the user:

1. stop
2. understand
3. trust
4. act

## Repertoire

Excellent designers build judgment by studying high-quality references continuously.

Useful sources include:

- product interfaces
- UI libraries
- design galleries
- strong commercial pages
- proven app patterns

The goal is not imitation. The goal is pattern recognition.

## Practical Standard

When exploring direction, reference what already works in comparable contexts:

- proven onboarding flows
- strong conversion pages
- clear CTA patterns
- established layout systems

Broad repertoire improves speed, originality, and decision quality.
