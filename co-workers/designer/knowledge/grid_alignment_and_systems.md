# Grid Alignment And Systems

> **Objective:** Create professional-looking interfaces through alignment, spacing consistency, and reusable patterns.

Strong design quality is often perceived through order before the user consciously identifies why.

## Grid And Alignment

A professional interface should have:

- a clear grid
- consistent margins
- predictable spacing
- aligned edges and baselines

Misalignment, uneven spacing, and drifting margins make the product feel unpolished immediately.

## System Thinking

Excellent designers think in systems, not isolated screens.

This means:

- standardized buttons
- consistent colors
- reusable components
- recurring patterns for states and layouts
- a shared design logic that scales

## Why It Matters

Systems improve:

- implementation speed
- product consistency
- user familiarity
- scalability across screens and features

## Review Questions

- Does the screen follow a visible structural logic?
- Are margins and spacing intervals consistent?
- Are components reusable rather than one-off?
- Does the design feel like part of a product system?
